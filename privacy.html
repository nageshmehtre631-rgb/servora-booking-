Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Servora is a website where customers book home service workers such as plumbers and electricians. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and the choices you have.

1. Information we collect

  • Google account details: your name, email address and profile name when you sign in with Google. We never see your Google password.
  • Mobile number: entered by you when you register as a worker or make a booking. We do not verify it by OTP.
  • Worker profile: service, state, city, area, price per visit and years of experience.
  • Booking details: service, date, time slot, address, a note about the problem, and booking status.
  • Location: only if you tap “Use my location”. We save your city and an approximate position (rounded to about 1 km). Your exact position is not stored.
  • Device storage: your chosen city is saved in your browser so you do not have to enter it again.

2. How we use it

  • To let you sign in and to create and manage bookings.
  • To show customers workers in their city, with approximate distance.
  • To let customers and workers contact each other about a booking.
  • To prevent misuse and improve the service.

3. Who can see your information

  • Everyone: a worker’s name, service, city, area, price, experience and approximate location.
  • Signed-in customers: a worker’s mobile number is shared when they book that worker.
  • The worker you book: your name, mobile number, address and booking note.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for advertising.

4. Services we use

  • Google Firebase (sign-in and database) stores your data on Google’s servers.
  • Netlify hosts this website.
  • OpenStreetMap Nominatim turns your location into a city name when you use the location button. Your coordinates are sent to that service for this purpose.

These providers have their own privacy policies.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account and bookings are active. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

6. Your rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or withdraw your consent, as allowed under Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Email us at the address below and we will respond within a reasonable time.

7. Security

We use Firebase security rules and HTTPS to protect data. No online service is completely secure, so please do not share information you are not comfortable providing.

8. Children

Servora is meant for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.
